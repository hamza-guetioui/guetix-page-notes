Questions

My note is gone after the page changed

Pages that change their address count as new pages. Find every note in All notes.

A shortcut does nothing

Another app may use those keys. Pick your own at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Notes don’t show on a page

Browser pages (chrome://, Web Store, PDFs) block extensions. Check “Show notes on pages” in Settings.

Move notes to another computer

Settings → Export backup, then Import it there.
